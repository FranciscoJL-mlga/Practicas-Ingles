Modal verbs

Practica el uso de los verbos modales simples y sus formas perfectas. Lee la teoría y elige un ejercicio.

Modales simples — usos
ModalUsoEjemplo
must / have toObligaciónYou must wear a seatbelt
must notProhibiciónYou must not smoke here
do not have toNo es necesarioYou do not have to come
should / ought toConsejoYou should see a doctor
can / couldHabilidad presente / pasadoShe could swim at five
may / mightPosibilidadIt might rain tomorrow
mustDeducción positivaHe must be tired
cannotDeducción negativaShe cannot be home
Modales perfectos — forma + significado
FormaSignificadoEjemplo
must have + p.p.Deducción pasada (segura)She must have left already
cannot have + p.p.Deducción negativa pasadaHe cannot have done it
should have + p.p.Crítica o arrepentimientoYou should have called
could have + p.p.Posibilidad pasada no realizadaI could have helped
might have + p.p.Posibilidad pasada débilThey might have forgotten
need not have + p.p.Acción pasada innecesariaYou need not have bought flowers
