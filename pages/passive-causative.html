Passive and causative

The passive and the causative

Practica la voz pasiva y la estructura causativa con ejercicios de completar, opción múltiple y corrección de errores. Lee la teoría y elige un ejercicio.

Voz pasiva — formación
TiempoFormaEjemplo
Present simpleis / are + past participleThe car is washed
Past simplewas / were + past participleThe car was washed
Present continuousis / are being + past participleThe car is being washed
Present perfecthas / have been + past participleThe car has been washed
Future simplewill be + past participleThe car will be washed
Modalmodal + be + past participleThe car must be washed
Estructura causativa
EstructuraUsoEjemplo
have + obj + past participleAlguien lo hace por tiI had my car repaired
get + obj + past participleIgual (más informal)I got my hair cut
have + person + bare infinitiveHacer que alguien lo hagaI had him repair the car
get + person + to-infinitiveConvencer a alguienI got him to repair the car
